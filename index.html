Privacy Policy – Little Order

Android app (com.daynest.daynest)

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

Little Order keeps your notes, to-dos, meetings and reminders on your own phone, encrypted. We do not run any server, we do not collect your data, and we never share or sell it. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you choose to use an optional feature described below.

Who we are

Little Order is developed by Mauro Todini, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy applies only to the Little Order app.

Information stored on your phone

  • Your notes, to-dos, meetings, reminders, completed items and settings are saved only on your device.
  • This data is encrypted with AES-256 using a key kept in the Android Keystore of your phone.
  • Android cloud backup of the app is turned off, so your data is not copied to Google Drive by the app.

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any account login.

PIN and fingerprint

The optional app PIN is stored only on your phone as a salted, one-way hash. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android itself; the app never receives or stores your biometric data.

Optional features that use your data

  • AI assistant (Google Gemini) – off unless you add your own key. If you paste your own Gemini API key in Settings and use an AI action (for example summarise, tidy up, or turn a note into to-dos), the text of that note or entry is sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini service to create the answer. It is not sent to us. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms. Your API key is stored only on your phone, and you can remove it in Settings at any time.
  • Calendar – off unless you turn it on. If you connect a calendar, the app asks for calendar permission. It reads the list of calendars on your phone so you can choose one, and adds or updates events for your Little Order entries in that calendar. If that calendar belongs to your Google account, Google Calendar may sync those events under your account. The app does not read your other calendar events for any other purpose.
  • Backups. When you export a backup, it is protected with a password you choose and saved only where you pick (for example your Downloads folder). We never receive it.
  • Print / Save as PDF. Printing uses the standard Android print service and goes only to the printer or file you choose.

Notifications and alarms

The app uses notification, exact alarm, boot and battery permissions only so your meeting and reminder alerts appear on time, including after your phone restarts. This happens entirely on your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not send your data to us or to any server of ours.
  • We do not share or sell data to third parties.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries.
  • The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Google Play

When you download the app, Google Play may collect information as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from this, apart from anonymous, combined statistics that Google Play Console provides to all developers (such as install numbers).

Deleting your data

Because your data stays on your device, you control it fully. You can delete single entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or uninstalling the app. Events already added to your calendar can be deleted in your calendar app. You can remove your Gemini key in Settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
